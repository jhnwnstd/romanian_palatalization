  audit TRIGGER-ABSENT  this module TRIGGER-ABSENT   182
  Differences: (a) the audit's detector takes the z of the -ez-
  augment for the palatal reflex of d (arendezi, legendezi:
  ALTERNATES); here d + -ez- is a coronal before /e/, not a trigger;
  (b) the audit counts any cell with the reflex (amănunțești,
  îmbogățești: ț before the -esc- augment), here only cells whose
  vowel triggers the class's rule count, so those are evidence of the
  -e cell; (c) the audit reads every augmented cell as TRIGGER-ABSENT,
  here a dorsal before -ez- (blochezi) meets /e/ unpalatalized:
  NO-ALTERNATION; (d) NO-READING: the verb's stem does not end in the
  noun's target (picur-a, strunj-i, micșor-a), so no cell is read.

7. Doublets (R19)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  noun lemmas with more than one lexicon row: 4
  noun lemmas whose rows list different plurals: 0
  Each root keeps the plural of its priority row (a row with a
  plural first), so with no conflicts every root has at most one
  plural and the three doublet codings give identical E0 rows.
  Doublets arise only from E1/E2 evidence (verb cells, derivatives).
