Romanian spell-out TP (design R20) — harmonized comparison
========================================================================

Question: do roots whose plural alternates (underspecified with
certainty) take the palatalizing verbalizer -i rather than -a/-ui,
as the spell-out account predicts? Unit: root types. N = certain
roots with an attested derived verb from the pair -i vs -a/-ui;
e = roots taking only -a/-ui; roots with both are doublets coded by
corpus share (dominant >= 90% decides; R19). theta = N/ln N.
Attestation: plural and verb (infinitive + participle tokens) at tau.

1. The critique's numbers, reproduced (check_claims.py definitions)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  full lexicon: 164 verbalizer pairs on alternating-plural
    dorsals (-i 91, -a 53, -ui 7, -ări 6, -iza 4, -ăni 2, -arisi 1);
    non-palatalizing pairs (-a, -ui) = 60 vs theta(164) = 32.2
    as root types (-i/-a/-ui only): 129 roots = 72 -i only + 43 -a/-ui only + 14 both
  top-1000: 42 verbalizer pairs on alternating-plural
    dorsals (-i 20, -a 15, -iza 2, -ui 2, -arisi 1, -ăni 1, -ări 1);
    non-palatalizing pairs (-a, -ui) = 17 vs theta(42) = 11.2
    as root types (-i/-a/-ui only): 28 roots = 12 -i only + 8 -a/-ui only + 8 both

  Differences from the harmonized definition below: pairs vs roots;
  n includes suffixes outside the competing pair (-ări, -iza, -ăni,
  -arisi); dorsals only; no attestation threshold; the top-1000 frame
